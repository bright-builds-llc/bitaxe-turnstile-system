# Deferred V2 Design Areas

These areas are intentionally outside v1 and require their own design decisions before implementation.

- Tiered Telemetry History with longer retention, export, deletion, and explicit normalized-work billing rates.
- Digital Energy Credits and account-bound balances representing previously verified Bitcoin-productive work.
- Recurring service-work billing or subscriptions.
- Continuous post-gate mining products.
- PPLNS or other pooled reward accounting.
- Multi-user Operator and Viewer Control Grants.
- Remote voltage, frequency, fan, thermal, Wi-Fi, and pool configuration.
- Optional irreversible device-hardening ceremonies.
- Independently verifiable cryptographic work receipts.

Any work-priced tier uses exact normalized work and a separate Work Consent; it never bills by raw share count or hides mining inside ordinary device operation.
