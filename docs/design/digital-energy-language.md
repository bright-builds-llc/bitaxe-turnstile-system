# Digital Energy Language

“Digital Energy” is a human-facing metaphor reserved for future Digital Energy Credit experiences. It describes electrical effort applied through Bitcoin mining and evidenced as Bitcoin-productive proof of work even when the work does not find a network-valid block.

## Required guardrails

- Exact normalized expected hashes remain the authoritative accounting unit.
- Equivalent Binary-Zero Work remains the preferred human-readable difficulty approximation.
- Digital Energy is not measured in joules or kilowatt-hours because hardware efficiency varies.
- Digital Energy is not stored electricity, Bitcoin, a mining reward, or guaranteed economic value.
- A Digital Energy Credit is an issuer-defined account-bound service record, not automatically a transferable currency or financial claim.
- V1 Gate Passes remain fresh, action-bound, short-lived authorizations and never become banked credits automatically.

## Suggested user-facing explanation

> Bitcoin mining applies electrical energy to search valid Bitcoin block candidates. Even when a hash does not find a block, accepted results can prove that useful Bitcoin-compatible work was performed. We call that measured work “digital energy” as an analogy; BWG accounts for it in exact expected hashes, not units of electricity.
