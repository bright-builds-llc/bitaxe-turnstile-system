# Minimize identity across contexts

The Relying Service will receive only an opaque Action Reference and Gate Pass; the Mining Pool will receive opaque Work Session and necessary payout data; Worker Management will receive an opaque Work Lease; and the Gate Authority will not require an Account Identity or persistent Device Identity. Claimant keys will be fresh and pairwise, payout-address correlation will be disclosed, and records will expire or become non-identifying aggregates once replay, dispute, and operational needs end.
