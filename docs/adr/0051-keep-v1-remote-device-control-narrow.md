# Keep v1 remote device control narrow

Remote v1 management will expose health and mining status, Work Lease dispatch and cancellation, Mining Baseline restoration, ordinary pause/resume, identify and rename, signed OTA, and Owner Grant administration. Voltage, frequency, fan and thermal policy, Wi-Fi and pool secrets, factory reset, irreversible hardening, and sensitive raw logs remain local-only until separate safety, privacy, and recovery evidence supports remote parity.
