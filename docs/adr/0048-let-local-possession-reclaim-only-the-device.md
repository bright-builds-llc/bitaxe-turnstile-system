# Let local possession reclaim only the device

When all account authenticators are lost, a new local Pairing Ceremony may perform Device Reclamation by rotating identity when needed, revoking the inaccessible Owner Grant, and assigning that one Worker to a new Account Identity while preserving safe mining settings. The device cannot recover the prior account, other Workers, payout data, identifiers, history, or unrelated services, limiting the effect of theft or resale.
