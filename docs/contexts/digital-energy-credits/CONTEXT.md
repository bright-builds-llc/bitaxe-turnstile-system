# Digital Energy Credits

This deferred v2 context describes account-bound service credits backed by previously verified Bitcoin-productive work. It is not part of BWG Core v1 and does not model physical energy, Bitcoin balances, or transferable money.

## Language

**Digital Energy**:
A human-facing metaphor for electrical effort applied through Bitcoin mining and evidenced as useful Bitcoin-productive work, even when no Bitcoin block is found.
_Avoid_: Stored electricity, joules, Bitcoin, guaranteed economic value

**Digital Energy Credit**:
A future account-bound service credit recording an exact normalized quantity of previously verified Bitcoin-productive work for redemption under an explicit issuer policy.
_Avoid_: Gate Pass, mining share, bitcoin balance, transferable currency
