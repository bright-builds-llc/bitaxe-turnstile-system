Terms

These terms cover using your Bitaxe with this Gate deployment, run by Bright Builds LLC, to earn a Gate Pass.

What you agree to

When you choose Start, you let the Gate direct your miner's hashing to the pool shown on the consent screen until the challenge's work is done. The Gate does this through a Work Lease of at most 60 seconds, renewed every 20 seconds while your browser stays connected. Keep the page open; if it closes, the lease lapses. The lease says how much work to do, never how: your Bitaxe runs under its own saved settings for frequency, voltage and fan.

What you get

  • When enough accepted work is credited, a single-use Gate Pass for the one action you started, which the site has 2 minutes to redeem.
  • The pool offer you consent to declares that if your miner finds a block, all of its reward goes directly to the payout address you chose, with no pool fee and no service fee. That is the pool's undertaking: the Gate doesn't verify the pool's block template.

Accepted work creates no claim on future revenue and no balance held for you. There is no payment for work, and no guarantee that a share is accepted or that a payout happens.

When work doesn't count

  • Work that arrives late or isn't eligible is refused and doesn't count.
  • A challenge lasts 15 minutes. Pause keeps credited work; Cancel ends the challenge and its progress can't be resumed.
  • If the site's action fails after you have a pass, the work isn't refunded.
  • If the Gate is unavailable, it refuses rather than guesses; the site may offer another way.

Your device
